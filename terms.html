What Intercede is

A free app in which a community shares prayer requests and prays for one another, with a daily prayer focus written by the community's administrator. It is offered by William Scott as the mobile companion to the BibleBing website, with which it shares accounts and the prayer wall.

Your account

You sign in with a Google account that you are entitled to use. You are responsible for what is shared from it. You must be at least 13.

What you may share

Prayer requests — your own, or on behalf of someone who would be glad to have people praying for them. Please do not share:

  • other people's private information: full names alongside medical details, addresses, phone numbers, or anything they have not agreed to have made public;
  • abuse, harassment, threats, or content that attacks a person or a group;
  • advertising, fundraising links, or anything that is not a prayer request;
  • anything unlawful.

Everything you share is public and may be read by anyone on the BibleBing website.

Moderation

Any member can ask for a request to be removed, and a single such request currently takes a post down. The administrator can remove any request, restore a request removed in error, and suspend accounts that repeatedly break these terms. There is no appeal process beyond email, and no obligation to explain a decision.

Not medical, legal or crisis advice

Nothing in Intercede — a request, a prayer, an answered note, a daily focus — is medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and nobody is monitoring the app for emergencies. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.
