Intercede: Pray for One Another · Android

Intercede gives you one thing to pray for each day, and a community wall where people share what they are carrying and pray for each other. This page is where to get help with it.

It helps to include your Android version and your device. Please don't paste a prayer request into an email unless you are asking about that specific request.

Why do I have to sign in?

Two reasons, both about the community rather than about you. First, your requests need to be yours: only the person who shared a request can mark it answered or delete it, and that needs an account to check against. Second, the prayer count on each request should mean something — one person, one prayer — and without an account there is no way to know that.

Intercede uses Google sign-in only. It receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and nothing else. There is no Intercede password to remember or to leak.

Where does today's prayer come from?

The community's administrator writes the day's focus — a title, a prayer, and usually a verse. On days when nothing has been written, Intercede shows the prayer from that day's BibleBing devotional instead, so there is always something to pray for. The scripture text is taken from the same source.

Who can see my prayer request?

Everyone. A request you share is public: it appears in the app for every signed-in member, and on the prayer wall at https://biblebing.com/prayers, which anyone on the internet can read without an account. Please write with that in mind — don't include other people's full names, addresses, phone numbers, medical details or anything they would not want made public.

You can share anonymously: turn on Share anonymously in the composer and your request is shown as “Anonymous”. It is still linked to your account so you can manage it, but nobody else can see who wrote it, and that link is removed if you delete your account.

What does “Pray” actually do?

It tells the person who shared the request that someone prayed — the count next to the button goes up by one. That is the whole feature, and it is deliberately small. Intercede does not send the author a notification, does not reveal who prayed, and does not let you pray twice for the same request. A tap is a promise you make, not a like.

Asking for a request to be removed

Every request has Ask for removal in its menu. Intercede is a small community, so at the moment a single removal request takes a post down straight away — the same rule the website uses. Use it for anything abusive, spam, or that exposes someone's private details. Removed requests are kept for the administrator, who can restore anything taken down by mistake; email if that has happened to yours.

Marking a prayer answered

Open Mine, choose the request and tap Mark answered. You can add a short note about what happened. The request stays on the wall with an “Answered” banner so the people who prayed for it get to see the end of the story.

The daily reminder

Off by default. Turn it on in Profile and choose a time; Intercede sends a local notification at that time each day. On Android 13 and later the system asks you to allow notifications first. The reminder is scheduled on your phone — nothing is sent from a server — so it may arrive a few minutes late if the phone is in battery saver.

If you are in crisis

Intercede is a place to be prayed for, not a crisis service, and nobody is monitoring it in real time. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now — in the US, call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland, Samaritans on 116 123. Then, if you want to, share a request.

Deleting your requests or your account

A request: open Mine and tap Delete. It is taken off the wall immediately.

Your account: Profile → Delete account. This removes your streak, your check-ins, and the record of what you prayed for, and it detaches every request you shared from your account — they stay on the wall as “Anonymous”, because the people praying for them are still praying. If you would rather the requests themselves came down too, delete them from Mine first. You can also email williamscott701@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for the same thing.

Your BibleBing website account is shared with Intercede and is not deleted by this; see the website for that.

Intercede and BibleBing

Intercede is the mobile companion to BibleBing. They share one sign-in and one prayer wall: a request shared in the app appears on the website and vice versa, and the daily devotional the app falls back to is the website's. Progress in the website's reading plans and quizzes is not shown in Intercede.
