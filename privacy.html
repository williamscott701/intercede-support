Privacy Policy

Intercede for Android · Last updated September 22, 2026

Intercede is a community app, so unlike a private journal some of what you write in it is meant to be seen. This page says exactly what is collected, what is public, and how to remove it.

What Intercede collects

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in, Google sends Intercede your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier. Intercede does not receive your Google password, contacts, calendar or anything else.
  • What you share. The text of each prayer request, whether you chose to share it anonymously, and any note you add when marking it answered.
  • What you do. Which requests you prayed for, which you asked to have removed, and the days you tapped “I prayed” on the day's focus (this is what your streak is made of).
  • Your reminder setting. Whether the daily reminder is on and at what time. This stays on your phone.

Intercede contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect your location, your contacts, or device identifiers. The server keeps ordinary request logs (IP address, time, endpoint) for a short period to keep the service running and to blunt abuse.

What is public

A prayer request you share is public. It is shown to every member in the app, and on the prayer wall of the BibleBing website, which anyone can read without an account. With it goes either your first name or “Anonymous”, the time it was shared, how many people prayed, and later whether you marked it answered and what you wrote.

Nothing else is public. Who prayed for a request is never shown. Your email address, your streak and your check-ins are visible only to you.

How it is used

To run the app: to show your requests as yours, to count each person's prayer once, to compute your streak, and to let the community's administrator moderate the wall. Your email address is used to tell you apart from other members and, if you write in, to reply to you. It is not used for marketing and there is no mailing list.

Where it is stored

In the same database that runs the BibleBing website, hosted on MongoDB Atlas, with the application server hosted on Vercel. Both are in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by those providers. Your sign-in session is stored on your phone in the app's private storage; the last screen you saw is cached there too so the app can open offline.

Who it is shared with

  • Other members and the public, for requests, as described above.
  • The community administrator, who can see every request including removed ones, and can see the name on requests that were not shared anonymously. The administrator cannot see who prayed for what.
  • Google, for sign-in, under Google's privacy policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas and Vercel, as hosting providers acting on Intercede's instructions.

Your data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists, or until you delete it. Requests you delete are hidden immediately and kept only in a removed state so that the administrator can undo mistaken removals; they are not shown anywhere. Server logs are kept for a few weeks at most.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete account removes your check-ins, your streak and the record of what you prayed for, and detaches every request you shared from your account so it remains on the wall as “Anonymous”. Delete individual requests from Mine first if you want them taken down as well.

By email: write to williamscott701@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and the same will be done for you, including taking down any requests you name. Because BibleBing and Intercede share one account, the website account itself is not deleted by the app; ask in the same email if you want that too.

Children

Intercede is for adults and for young people of 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, email and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous wording stays available in this page's public history. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app.
